## Frozen scope

The frozen campaign includes:

- causal backtesting and look-ahead controls;
- execution costs, delays, slippage, market impact and capacity tooling;
- PSR, DSR, multiple-testing correction, CSCV/PBO and block bootstrap;
- stationarity, drift, regimes, sensitivity, ablation and data resilience;
- tail risk, Monte Carlo, VaR and Expected Shortfall backtesting;
- temporal-dependence-adjusted Sharpe analysis;
- historical and counterfactual reverse stress;
- drawdown depth, duration, recovery and time-under-water analysis;
- controlled public exports, manifests and SHA-256 evidence commitments.

## Prohibition on further historical optimization

No additional historical optimization, champion selection, benchmark expansion,
parameter search or metric-driven model modification is authorized within
v0.3.0.

## Conditions for reopening

The frozen research campaign may be reopened only following:

1. a material data defect;
2. a material implementation defect;
3. a governance-approved model change;
4. new live or pilot evidence requiring formal outcome analysis.

Routine shadow/live monitoring does not by itself alter the frozen historical
research record.

## Proprietary boundary

The freeze does not authorize publication of daily Nostra returns, positions,
signals, features, coefficients, thresholds, private configurations or internal
research artifacts.
